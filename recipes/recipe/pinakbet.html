Pinakbet

Pinakbet is another dish that Filipinos love to eat with rice. Main ingredients mostly are mixed vegetables, shrimp paste, and pork. The saltiness of bagoong brings a balanced sweetness with ampalaya and other vegetables.

This recipe are loved by many elderlies as it is healthy and perfect with steamed rice.

Ingredients

  • ½ lb Pork Belly (1-inch cubes)
  • 1 tbsp Shrimp Paste
  • ½ small Kalabasa (squash), cut into pieces
  • 8 pcs Okra
  • ½ bunch Long Beans (3-inch lengths)
  • 1 medium Ampalaya (bitter melon), sliced
  • 1 large Eggplant, sliced
  • 2 Roma Tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 medium Onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves Garlic, minced
  • 2 cups Water
  • Salt and Pepper to taste
  • 1 tbsp Oil

Steps to Prepare

  1. Saute:
    Heat oil in a pot over medium heat. Add chopped onion and minced garlic. Cook, stirring regularly, until softened and fragrant.
  2. Brown the pork:
    Add the pork belly cubes. Cook, stirring occasionally, until lightly browned.
  3. Cook shrimp paste:
    Add the shrimp paste. Continue cooking, stirring occasionally, until it starts to brown (this reduces the "fishy" taste).
  4. Add tomatoes:
    Put in the chopped tomatoes. Cook, mashing with the back of a spoon, until softened and have released juice.
  5. Simmer:
    Pour in the water and bring to a boil. Lower heat, cover, and cook for about 15 to 20 minutes, or until the pork is tender. Add more water in ½ cup increments as needed to maintain about 1 cup of liquid.
  6. Add squash:
    Add the peeled and cut kalabasa pieces. Cook for about 2 minutes, or until almost tender.
  7. Add long beans:
    Add the long beans (cut into 3-inch lengths). Continue to cook until tender-crisp.
  8. Add remaining vegetables:
    Add ampalaya, eggplant, and okra. Continue to cook for about 4 to 5 minutes, or until vegetables are tender yet crisp.
  9. Season and serve:
    Add salt and pepper to taste. Stir, then turn off the heat. Transfer to a serving plate and enjoy hot with steamed rice.
